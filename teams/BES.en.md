### Founding

Beşiktaş is one of the most historic sports clubs in Turkey, with roots going back to 1903. The men's basketball section, in the form we know today, has operated since 1967 and is part of Istanbul's big three alongside Fenerbahçe and Galatasaray. The "Black Eagles", as they are known, have tied their modern history to the Akatlar arena, a venue small in size but known for its fiery atmosphere. The black-and-white colours and the eagle on the crest carry a working-class identity that has travelled from football onto the hardwood.

In basketball, Beşiktaş lived for years in the shadow of their great rivals. Anadolu Efes and Fenerbahçe dominated the last few decades, but the black-and-whites managed at certain moments to stir things up and write their own chapters.

### European titles

Beşiktaş's greatest European moment came in 2012, when they won the EuroChallenge, the FIBA Europe competition that was then the third tier in Europe. In the final of the Debrecen Final Four the team beat France's Élan Chalon 91-86, claiming its first and to date only European title. That success, combined with the league title, earned them a place in the 2012-13 EuroLeague.

After years without major European runs, Beşiktaş returned to the spotlight in the 2025-26 season. In the EuroCup they made it all the way to the final, where they lost the series 2-0 to Bourg (60-72 in Istanbul), with the second game decided in the final seconds (73-71).

### Domestic titles

In Turkey, Beşiktaş have won 2 league titles, in 1975 and 2012, 1 Cup (2012) and 1 Presidential Cup (2012). The first title in 1975 stood for decades as the club's great moment, while the team often found itself one step short of the top: it has finished runner-up in the league nine times in all, including in 2025 and 2026.

The 2011-12 season was the most glittering in the club's history. Beşiktaş won the Cup, the EuroChallenge and the league, beating Anadolu Efes in the finals, and a few months later added the Presidential Cup, again against Efes.

### Legends

- **Erman Kunter**: one of the biggest names in Turkish basketball, he wore the shirt in different periods and later sat on the team's bench.
- **Efe Aydan**: the big man considered one of the greatest Turkish players of all time, who spent the 1986-87 season at Beşiktaş.
- **Deron Williams**: arrived during the 2011 NBA lockout and left such a mark that the club retired the number 8.
- **Carlos Arroyo**: the leader of the 2012 team, MVP of the league finals.
- **Pops Mensah-Bonsu**: dominant in the paint in the 2011-12 season and MVP of the EuroChallenge Final Four.
- **Allen Iverson**: the club's highest-profile signing up to that point, in 2010, although injuries prevented him from leaving a mark on the court.
- **Alperen Şengün**: in a Beşiktaş shirt he was named MVP of the Turkish league in the 2020-21 season, before the leap to the NBA.

### Coaches

Ateş Çubukçu and American Tom Davis were associated with the 1975 title. Ergin Ataman was the architect of the 2012 season, when Beşiktaş won the league, the Cup and the EuroChallenge. Ahmet Kandemir took charge of the team in three separate spells, while since 2023 Serbian Dušan Alimpijević has built a team that has reached consecutive finals in Turkey and the EuroCup final.

### Today

Beşiktaş return to the EuroLeague in the 2026-27 season, 13 years after their previous appearance. Their entry was unanimously approved by the competition's shareholders after Bourg, the EuroCup winners, chose to stay in the EuroCup and did not take up their place, and so the black-and-whites became the EuroLeague's 20th team. The team must now prove that its consecutive finals were no fluke and that it can compete against Europe's elite.
