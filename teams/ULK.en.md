### Founding

Fenerbahçe basketball has roots going back to 1913, when the club from the Asian side of Istanbul created its section, while its present form took shape in 1944. The team's home is the Ülker Sports and Event Hall in Ataşehir, a modern arena of around 13,800 seats that fills with yellow and blue for every big game. For decades "Fener" was better known for its football, but from the mid-2000s onwards it turned into one of the great powers of European basketball.

### European titles

Fenerbahçe have won the EuroLeague twice. The first title came in 2017, at the Final Four in Istanbul, with an 80-64 win over Olympiacos in the final and Ekpe Udoh named MVP of the event. It was the first time a Turkish club had reached the top of Europe. The second was written in 2025 in Abu Dhabi, where the Turks beat Monaco 81-70 and Nigel Hayes-Davis was named Final Four MVP. That season ended with a treble, as the league and the Turkish Cup also followed.

The team's record also includes two lost finals: in 2016 in Berlin to CSKA Moscow after overtime and in 2018 in Belgrade to Real Madrid. In all, Fenerbahçe have made eight Final Four appearances, five in a row from 2015 to 2019 and three consecutive from 2024 to 2026.

### Domestic titles

As of 2026 Fenerbahçe have won 13 titles in the Turkish Super League, the last three in a row in 2024, 2025 and 2026, when they beat Beşiktaş in the finals. Adding the three league titles from the era before the league was established, the club reaches 16 national titles. They also have ten Turkish Cups and nine Presidential Cups. In 2025-26 they won all three domestic trophies.

### Legends

- **İbrahim Kutluay**: one of the most talented Turkish scorers of his generation, with a special place in the hearts of the club's fans.
- **Mirsad Türkcan**: the powerful forward of the 2006-2012 period, twice the EuroLeague's leading rebounder, with his number 6 shirt retired.
- **Ömer Onan**: the guard who played for Fener until 2014 and won many league titles, with his number 7 shirt retired.
- **Melih Mahmutoğlu**: the captain and the only player to have won both EuroLeagues in the team's shirt.
- **Bogdan Bogdanović**: the Serbian guard who was one of the protagonists of the 2017 title.
- **Ekpe Udoh**: the centre who anchored the defence and MVP of the 2017 Final Four.
- **Jan Veselý**: the Czech big man who wore the yellow shirt for years and won the first European title.
- **Gigi Datome**: the Italian forward with a pivotal role in the team of the golden five-year spell.
- **Kostas Sloukas**: the Greek point guard who won the 2017 EuroLeague with Fener.
- **Nigel Hayes-Davis**: the leader of the second title and MVP of the 2025 Final Four.

### Coaches

Željko Obradović changed the club's stature: in the seven years from 2013 to 2020 he led Fener to five straight Final Fours, three consecutive finals and the historic 2017 title. Šarūnas Jasikevičius added the second star in 2025, writing his name among the few who have won the EuroLeague both as players and as coaches. From the old era, Samim Göreç led the team to the Istanbul League titles in the mid-1950s.

### Today

Fenerbahçe are now one of the teams that set the standard in the EuroLeague. Steady investment, the presence of top-tier coaches and one of the most vibrant crowds in Europe keep them constantly among the favourites. As 2025 European champions and with three consecutive Final Fours, they have proved that their success is not circumstantial but the product of a plan.
