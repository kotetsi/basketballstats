### Founding

Dubai are the EuroLeague's newest team and at the same time one of its most ambitious. The club was founded in 2023 on the initiative of Emirati businessman Abdulla Saeed Juma Al Naboodah and Dejan Kamenjašević, who also took on the role of general manager. The aim was clear from the outset: to give the United Arab Emirates a team capable of competing at the highest European level and acting as a driver for the growth of basketball across the Gulf region. An academy was set up around the first team, and in 2024 a partnership was signed with local club Shabab Al Ahli.

Its home is the Coca-Cola Arena, a modern venue in the centre of Dubai that can hold around 15,000 spectators and is used to hosting concerts and major international events. From the very first season, home games often drew more than 5,000 fans, a significant figure for a team with no tradition.

### The ABA League years

To build a competitive base, Dubai joined the Adriatic League in 2024 on a three-year deal, an unprecedented venture for the competition. The debut came on 22 September 2024 with a home win over Crvena Zvezda, and the team quickly showed it had not come just to make up the numbers. In its first season it finished third in the regular season, with 25 wins from 30 games, before being knocked out in the semi-finals by Partizan.

The second year was historic. Dubai finished first in the regular season, eliminated Spartak Subotica and Budućnost in the play-offs and in the finals once again faced Partizan. This time the series ended 3-1 in the Emiratis' favour, with the fourth game decided 83-81 in Belgrade thanks to two late free throws from Aleksa Avramović. It was the first title in the club's history.

### Joining the EuroLeague

In June 2025 the EuroLeague confirmed Dubai's participation with a five-year licence, as part of the competition's expansion from 18 to 20 teams. It thus became the first team from the United Arab Emirates to play in Europe's top competition. It played its first game at home against Partizan and celebrated a win, and in its debut season, played alongside the ABA League, it finished 11th with a balanced 19-19 record, just outside the play-in zone. For a two-year-old team it was a showing that earned the respect of its opponents.

### Key figures

- **Nate Mason**: the first signing in the club's history.
- **Dāvis Bertāns**: the first player with NBA experience to wear the team's shirt, with shooting as his main weapon.
- **Klemen Prepelič**: the Slovenian guard who became the team's captain.
- **Aleksa Avramović**: the Serbian international whose free throws decided the ABA League title.
- **Mfiondu Kabengele**: the Canadian big man who was named MVP of the 2026 finals.
- **Justin Anderson, McKinley Wright and Dwayne Bacon**: players with NBA pasts who added quality in the first European season.

### Coaches

Croatian Jurica Golemac was the team's first coach and guided it through its first steps, both in the ABA League and for most of the debut EuroLeague season, before leaving in April 2026. He was succeeded by Slovenian Aleksander Sekulić, who finished the season with the ABA League title. Since July 2026 the bench has been occupied by Xavi Pascual, one of the most experienced coaches in Europe, with spells at Barcelona, Panathinaikos and Zenit.

### Today

Dubai enter their second EuroLeague season as ABA League champions and with a clear intention to step up a level. The hiring of Pascual and the signing of Tornike Shengelia show that the management wants to move quickly from the phase of "curious newcomer" to that of a team competing for a play-off place. Its future will depend on whether it can build its own fan base and identity, so that it does not remain merely an expensive experiment.
