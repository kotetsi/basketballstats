### Founding

Paris are the newest team in the EuroLeague and perhaps the fastest rise story in modern European basketball. The club was announced in July 2018, with the project led by American David Kahn, former head of basketball operations at the Minnesota Timberwolves, and backed by American capital. The idea was simple but ambitious: to finally give the French capital a basketball team worthy of its size. From 2018 to 2024 Paris played at the small Halle Georges Carpentier, with around 5,000 seats, while since 2024 its home has been the Adidas Arena, an 8,000-seat venue built for the Olympic Games. For big games it also uses the Accor Arena.

### First steps

From the start the team bet on young players and a fast, attacking style. Under Jean-Christophe Prat, who was on the bench from 2018 to 2022, talents such as Juhann Bégarin and Ismaël Kamagate emerged, selected in the NBA Draft in 2021 and 2022 respectively. Paris started out in Pro B, the second division, and went up to the top flight in 2021, finishing second. Within two years of promotion it had already become one of the most interesting teams in the country.

### European titles

The 2023-24 season changed everything. With Finland's Tuomas Iisalo on the bench, Paris won the EuroCup, beating French side Bourg 2-0 in the finals, and secured access to the EuroLeague. Iisalo was named the competition's coach of the year and TJ Shorts its MVP. In its EuroLeague debut, in the 2024-25 season, the team surprised Europe: with Tiago Splitter on the bench it finished eighth in the regular season at 19-15, beat Real 81-73 in Madrid in the play-in and reached the play-offs, where it was swept 3-0 by Fenerbahçe. The second season, 2025-26, was harder: Italian Francesco Tabellini was dismissed in March, 28-year-old Julius Thomas, the youngest coach in EuroLeague history, took over on an interim basis, and Paris finished 16th at 15-23.

### Domestic titles

The first domestic trophy came in February 2024, with the Leaders Cup and a win over Nanterre in the final. The same year Paris reached the league finals, where they lost to Monaco. Revenge came in 2025: first they won the French Cup, beating Le Mans 91-80, and then, in a series decided 99-93 in the fifth game, they beat Monaco to celebrate the first league title in their history. In 2026 the two teams met in the finals for the third year in a row, and this time Monaco took the fifth game 101-92 at the Adidas Arena, leaving Paris in second place.

### Key players

TJ Shorts is the face of the rise, the diminutive point guard who was EuroCup MVP, Leaders Cup MVP and a protagonist of the first league title, before moving to Panathinaikos in 2025. Nadir Hifi developed into one of the best French scorers of his generation. Sebastian Herrera, the team captain, and Tyson Ward were key parts of the EuroCup triumph, along with Collin Malcolm. In the early period Kamagate and Bégarin stood out, showing that Paris can produce players.

### Today

In just a few years Paris have gone from the second division to the EuroLeague, with a European title, a league title and a Cup in the trophy cabinet. The French capital's market, the new arena and their attacking basketball make them one of the most attractive teams in the competition. For the 2026-27 season the club is betting on Max Lefevre, a former assistant coach of the Minnesota Timberwolves. The question now is not whether they belong in the EuroLeague, but how quickly they can become regular contenders.
