### Founding

Barcelona's basketball section was founded on 24 August 1926 and is one of the oldest in Spain. Since 1971 its home has been the Palau Blaugrana, an arena of around 7,500 seats next to the Camp Nou, small by today's standards but with an atmosphere that recalls other eras. The team's identity has been built on two pillars: the great rivalry with Real Madrid and an academy, La Masia, which has produced some of the finest Spanish players of all time.

### European titles

For years the EuroLeague was the Catalans' great sore point. The team lost five finals before finally lifting the trophy: in 1984 to Roma, in 1990 to Jugoplastika, in 1991 to Pop 84, in 1996 to Panathinaikos in Paris by a single point and in 1997 to Olympiacos in Rome. Redemption came in 2003 at the Palau Sant Jordi, with a win over Benetton. The second title followed in 2010 in Paris, against Olympiacos, at the end of a season with a 20-2 record. In 2021 Barça reached the final again but fell to Efes. In all, Barça have made 17 Final Four appearances. Their trophy cabinet also holds two Cup Winners' Cups (Saporta), in 1985 and 1986, two Korać Cups, in 1987 and 1999, and an Intercontinental Cup in 1985.

### Domestic titles

As of 2026 Barcelona have won 20 Spanish league titles, the most recent in 2021 and 2023. They have lifted the Copa del Rey 27 times, most recently in 2022, and also have six Super Cups, the last in 2015. The 2002-03 season went down in history as the first treble: EuroLeague, league and cup. Over the last three seasons the team has not added a major title, with the 2026 league final lost to Valencia.

### Legends

Juan Antonio San Epifanio, "Epi", was the team's scorer and voice in the 1980s and 1990s, and his number 15 has been retired. Nacho Solozábal, wearing 7, was the point guard of the same generation. Andrés Jiménez and Roberto Dueñas gave the paint muscle and stability, and their numbers are also no longer worn. American Audie Norris is remembered for his power under the boards. Dejan Bodiroga was the leader and MVP of the 2003 Final Four, with Šarūnas Jasikevičius pulling the strings alongside him. Juan Carlos Navarro, wearing 11, is perhaps the most beloved player in the club's history and the leader of the 2010 team. Pau Gasol came through the academy, became an NBA star and returned in 2021 to celebrate another league title. Ricky Rubio was the young talent who brought freshness to the team of the second European crown.

### Coaches

Aíto García Reneses, across successive spells, built the team that dominated Spain in the late 1980s and 1990s. Svetislav Pešić delivered the first EuroLeague and the 2003 treble. Xavi Pascual, a product of the club, won the second EuroLeague in 2010 and returned to the bench in November 2025 for just one season. Šarūnas Jasikevičius went from on-court legend to the coach of the 2021 and 2023 league titles. Since July 2026 the bench has belonged to Slovenian Aleksander Sekulić.

### Today

Barcelona remain one of the big names of the EuroLeague, with a strong budget and an academy that continues to feed the first team. In recent years, however, the club has gone through a transitional period with frequent changes on the bench, and in 2026 it was eliminated in the play-in by Monaco. Its absence from the Final Four since 2023 weighs on a fan base used to spending spring at the top. The goal is always the same: to become a title-winning team again.
