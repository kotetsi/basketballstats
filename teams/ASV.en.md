### Founding

ASVEL was born in 1948 from the merger of two clubs from the Lyon area, AS Villeurbanne and Éveil Lyonnais, and took its name from their initials. The club is based in Villeurbanne, the city that borders Lyon, and for decades the team was synonymous with French basketball. Since 1995 it has played at the Astroballe, while since November 2023 its big games have been staged at the modern LDLC Arena, which holds around 12,500. One fact says a great deal: since its founding, ASVEL has never been relegated. Since 2018, thanks to a ten-year sponsorship deal with the LDLC group, it has competed under the name LDLC ASVEL.

### European titles

ASVEL have never won a European trophy, but they have left their mark on several competitions. In the European Champions Cup they reached the semi-finals in 1976 and 1978. In 1983 they played in the Cup Winners' Cup final, where they lost 111-99 to Scavolini Pesaro. Their finest moment of the modern era was the 1997 Final Four in Rome: having knocked out Estudiantes and Efes in the knockout rounds, they lost the semi-final to Barcelona and the third-place game to Olimpija Ljubljana, finishing fourth. EuroLeague quarter-finals followed in 1999, 2000 and 2001.

### Domestic titles

In France, ASVEL are the most decorated club. As of 2026 they have a record 21 league titles, the first coming as early as 1949 and the most recent in 2019, 2021 and 2022. They have also won ten French Cups, the last in 2021, two Leaders Cups (2010, 2023) and two Super Cups (2009, 2016). In recent seasons the French crown has passed to Paris and Monaco, with ASVEL failing to reach the finals.

### Legends

Alain Gilles is "Mr ASVEL": eight league titles as a player, the club's all-time leading scorer with more than 6,000 points and its record appearance holder, with his number 4 retired. Henri Grange won seven league titles between 1955 and 1969. American Delaney Rudd was the brains of the 1997 team, is the club's all-time assists leader and the number 4 has also been retired in his honour. Amara Sy, wearing 5, was for years the captain and soul of the team. Tony Parker wore the shirt during the 2011 NBA lockout, before becoming president and principal shareholder. Théo Maledon came through the academy and went to the NBA, as did Zaccharie Risacher later on. Victor Wembanyama passed through Villeurbanne in the 2021-22 season, while Nando De Colo, a former EuroLeague MVP, gave the team experience and quality in the final years of his career.

### Coaches

André Buffière was the man of the early years, on the bench from the club's founding. Grégor Beugnot led the team to the 1997 Final Four and to cups in 1996, 1997 and 2001. Zvezdan Mitrović delivered the 2019 double, while T.J. Parker won the 2021 double, followed by the 2022 title. Since the 2026-27 season Tony Parker himself has been on the bench, succeeding Pierric Poupet.

### Today

With Tony Parker as president since 2014, Nicolas Batum a shareholder since 2017, the Olympique Lyonnais group, which acquired a stake in 2019, new investors who joined the shareholding in 2026 and the new arena, ASVEL have been a permanent EuroLeague presence since 2019. Results, however, have not matched the ambitions, and the team usually moves in the lower reaches of the standings, finishing last (8-30) in the 2025-26 season. The challenge is to turn its strong brand and academy into a team that can compete for the play-offs.
