### Founding

Bayern's basketball section was founded in 1946, when Franz Kronberger, a former footballer who had stopped playing because of a war injury, decided to bring the sport to Munich's biggest club. In 1966 it was one of the founding members of the Bundesliga and finished fourth in its first season, but in 1974, after eight years, it dropped out of the top flight. Decades in the shadow of the football team followed, spent mostly in the lower divisions. The revival began in 2011, when Dirk Bauermann's team won the ProA with seven rounds to spare and returned to the Bundesliga with the backing of the club's management. From September 2011 Bayern played at the renovated Audi Dome, the basketball venue of the 1972 Olympic Games, now called the BMW Park, while since October 2024 it has had a second home at the state-of-the-art SAP Garden in the Olympic Park, where it plays its European games.

### European titles

Bayern have yet to win a European trophy. Their EuroLeague history begins in 2013-14, two years after their return to the top flight, when they reached the Top 16 on their debut with Svetislav Pešić on the bench. Their finest moment came in 2021, when they qualified for the EuroLeague play-offs for the first time. There they took Olimpia Milano to a fifth game, narrowly missing out on a place in the Final Four. The following year they were back in the play-offs, against Barcelona, while in 2025 they reached the play-in, where they beat Crvena Zvezda in overtime but were eliminated by Real. In the 2025-26 season they finished 13th.

### Domestic titles

Bayern have seven German league titles. The first two came in 1954 and 1955, followed by a wait of almost six decades until the 2014 title. The rest came in 2018, 2019, 2024 and 2025. In the 2025-26 season they finished first in the regular season at 29-5, but lost the finals 3-2 to Alba Berlin, who in the fifth game at the SAP Garden came back from 20 points down at half-time to win 84-81. They have won the German Cup five times: in 1968, 2018, 2021, 2023 and 2024. Twice, in 2018 and 2024, they won the double.

### Legends

Steffen Hamann and Demond Greene, both with retired shirts, were key figures in the years of the return to the top. Malcolm Delaney was named MVP of the season and of the finals in the 2014 league title run, the one that ended 59 years of waiting. Nihad Đedović was the scorer who linked the first successful era with the second. Vladimir Lučić was the team's mainstay and captain for years, and a protagonist in the 2021 play-off run. Paul Zipser, a product of the German system who spent time in the NBA, and Danilo Barthel gave the team a German identity. Finally, Andreas Obst became one of the most dangerous shooters in Europe and a key part of the recent league titles.

### Coaches

Svetislav Pešić built the modern Bayern and gave them the historic 2014 title. Dejan Radonjić led the team to its first double in 2018 and the 2019 league title. Andrea Trinchieri made Bayern competitive in Europe, with the play-offs of 2021 and 2022 and two Cups. Pablo Laso added the second double in 2024 and Gordon Herbert the 2025 league title. When Herbert was dismissed in December 2025, Pešić returned for the rest of the season, while since the summer of 2026 the bench has belonged to Anton Gavel, captain of the 2018 double-winning team, who as Bamberg coach had won the 2026 Cup.

### Today

With the SAP Garden, the backing of one of the most powerful clubs in the world and a roster that is refreshed every summer, Bayern are now the flagship of German basketball in the EuroLeague. What they lack is the next step: a regular presence in the play-offs and, at some point, the first Final Four in their history.
