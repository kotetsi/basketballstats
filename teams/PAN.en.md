### Founding

Panathinaikos' basketball section was founded in 1919, within the multi-sport club born in Athens in 1908. It is one of the oldest organised basketball sections in Greece and, by some distance, the most successful at European level. Its home is the OAKA "Nikos Galis" Indoor Hall, today known as the Telekom Center Athens, one of the largest arenas in the EuroLeague. The "Shamrock" has built its identity on a rare culture of winning and the belief that every season is judged by trophies alone.

### European titles

Panathinaikos are seven-time European champions: in 1996, 2000, 2002, 2007, 2009, 2011 and 2024. The first trophy was won in Paris against Barcelona, in a final remembered for Stojko Vranković's block on the last play; the Intercontinental Cup followed the same year. Then came the most glittering period in the club's history, with five titles in twelve years, among them the 2007 final at OAKA in front of their own fans. After a long wait, in 2024 in Berlin the Greens beat Real Madrid 95-80 and returned to the top.

In all, the club has made 13 Final Four appearances, the most recent in 2025, and has lost only one final, in the 2001 SuproLeague. Its success rate in finals is among the most impressive in the competition's history.

### Domestic titles

As of 2026 Panathinaikos have 40 Greek league titles, a record for Greek basketball, the most recent in 2024. They also have 22 Greek Cups, the two most recent in 2025 and 2026, when they beat Olympiacos in the final. Their collection also includes a Super Cup, in 2021. The run from 1998 to 2011 holds a special place in their history, when league titles came almost every year.

### Legends

- **Dimitris Diamantidis**: three EuroLeagues, regular-season MVP in 2011 and his number 13 shirt retired for ever.
- **Fragiskos Alvertis**: his entire career with the Shamrock, five European titles and his number 4 hanging from the arena roof.
- **Dejan Bodiroga**: the Serbian who won two EuroLeagues and was MVP of the 2002 Final Four in Bologna.
- **Dominique Wilkins**: the NBA star who brought glamour and was MVP of the first Final Four, in 1996.
- **Stojko Vranković**: the big man of 1996, whose block sealed the first title.
- **Nikos Galis**: the greatest scorer in Greek basketball ended his career with the Shamrock in the early 1990s, giving his name to the arena.
- **Šarūnas Jasikevičius**: the Lithuanian point guard who brought his experience to the 2009 title.
- **Mike Batiste**: the tough big man who won three EuroLeagues in the green shirt.
- **Kostas Sloukas**: MVP of the 2024 Final Four and leader of the return to the top.
- **Kendrick Nunn**: the explosive scorer who was one of the protagonists of the seventh title.

### Coaches

Željko Obradović is the coach who became synonymous with the club, winning five EuroLeagues and a host of domestic titles in his 13 years in charge. Božidar Maljković wrote the first chapter with the 1996 trophy, while Ergin Ataman led the team to its seventh title in 2024. From an earlier era, Kostas Politis has his place among the most important coaches in the club's history.

### Today

Panathinaikos are one of the established powers of today's EuroLeague. With strong financial backing, a roster full of front-line names and one of the best-attended arenas in Europe, they enter every season aiming for the Final Four and an eighth title. The "eternal" derby with Olympiacos, now often decided at European level too, keeps the Greek presence at the top alive.
