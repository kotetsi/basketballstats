### Founding

Žalgiris was founded in 1944 in Kaunas and early on became much more than a basketball team. In the Soviet years its victories over CSKA Moscow carried deep national and emotional meaning for Lithuanians, and its name, which refers to the Battle of Grunwald, became a symbol of identity. Since 2011 its home has been the Žalgiris Arena, with a capacity of more than 15,000, which sells out for almost every game and is considered one of the loudest arenas in Europe.

### European titles

The greatest moment in the club's history came in 1999 in Munich. Žalgiris beat Olympiacos in the semi-final and Kinder Bologna 82-74 in the final to win the EuroLeague, with Tyus Edney named Final Four MVP. Just a year earlier, in 1998, they had also lifted the Saporta Cup. In the 1980s, Sabonis's team reached the European Champions Cup final in 1986, where they lost to Cibona, and the same year won the Intercontinental Cup. In the modern era, in 2018 they returned to the Final Four in Belgrade, where they lost to Fenerbahçe in the semi-final but took third place by beating CSKA 79-77. Their trophy cabinet also includes a North European Basketball League (NEBL) title in 1999.

### Domestic titles

In the USSR era Žalgiris won five league titles, in 1947, 1951 and three in a row from 1985 to 1987, against the dominance of CSKA, as well as a USSR Cup in 1953, while in the same years they also took nine titles in the regional championship of the Lithuanian SSR. After Lithuania's independence they became the undisputed power in the country. As of 2026 they have 26 Lithuanian league (LKL) titles, the latest in 2026 with a 3-0 sweep of Juventus, including eleven in a row from 2011 to 2021. They also have nine King Mindaugas Cups, with seven consecutive from 2020 to 2026, and five Baltic League titles.

### Legends

Arvydas Sabonis is the greatest player in the club's history and one of the finest centres of all time, the leader in the three Soviet league titles of the 1980s. Modestas Paulauskas was the team's first great star. Rimas Kurtinaitis and Valdemaras Chomičius were Sabonis's teammates who provided that generation's shooting and playmaking. Tyus Edney was the point guard and MVP of the 1999 Final Four, while Anthony Bowie was the team's top scorer in the final, with 17 points. Saulius Štombergas gave the same team strength and leadership. Paulius Jankūnas is the longest-serving player of the modern era, and his number 13 has been retired. Mantas Kalnietis and Edgaras Ulanovas were key parts of the 2018 Final Four team.

### Coaches

Vladas Garastas was on the bench for the Soviet league titles of 1985 to 1987. Jonas Kazlauskas led the team to the 1998 Saporta Cup and the 1999 European title. Šarūnas Jasikevičius, before becoming one of the most sought-after coaches in Europe, took Žalgiris to the 2018 Final Four. In recent years the baton has passed to Andrea Trinchieri and Tomas Masiulis.

### Today

With a smaller budget than Europe's giants, Žalgiris remain one of the most competitive teams in the EuroLeague thanks to discipline, sound management and the most fanatical home crowd in the competition. The team relies on a core of Lithuanian players and careful choices of foreign players, while Tomas Masiulis, a member of the 1999 team as a player and on the bench since the summer of 2025, continues the line of stability. Last season they finished fifth and reached the play-offs, where they were knocked out by Fenerbahçe, showing that Kaunas can dream of a Final Four again.
